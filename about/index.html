ABOUT ME

Business experience. Hands-on AI building.

I've spent more than 20 years leading operations, customer experience, and business transformation for enterprise clients. My work has included large teams, complex implementations, commercial ownership, and improving how people and systems work together.

Today I bring that experience into applied AI. I map workflows, build prototypes, connect tools, and test practical solutions. I'm interested in the space where business context, technology, and real adoption meet.

How I work

I connect business needs to practical systems through five steps, grounded in real workflows, useful outcomes, and evidence.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Start with the real workflow: the people, goals, current steps, and constraints.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    Find the friction or opportunity that matters and where meaningful value can be created.

  3. 03

    Build

    Turn the idea into a focused workflow, prototype, application, agent, or automation.

  4. 04

    Test

    Check it against representative scenarios, exceptions, safeguards, and day-to-day work.

  5. 05

    Measure

    Look for evidence in time, quality, capacity, adoption, customer outcomes, or business results.
