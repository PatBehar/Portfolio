About

I've spent more than 20 years leading operations, customer success, and business transformation — mostly the kind of work where you're responsible for making something complicated actually run: enterprise accounts, regional teams, technology rollouts that had to work the first time.

I'm not a developer. I never learned to code, and for most of my career that meant every product idea I had stayed an idea — I could design it, sell it, run it, but not build it. That's changed. I use AI as my technical team now: I direct, decide, and review; AI writes the code. AmpAI, the project featured on this site, is the real-time proof of that — no development team, no outsourced build, just me and AI shipping something real and documenting every decision along the way.

Why now: the gap between "I understand the business problem" and "I can ship the solution" used to require hiring a team. It doesn't anymore, if you're willing to actually direct the work instead of just prompting for it. That's the operating model this whole site is built on.
