Smart Lead Processing & Qualification

01 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

A clearer route from lead intake to CRM

Atlas Services · fictional scenario

Atlas Services is a fictional company, and every lead in this case is synthetic. Today, enquiries arrive through web forms and CSV files. Sales Operations checks contact details, interprets intent, assigns priority, and enters each lead by hand into a CRM. If a record is duplicated or an integration fails, the team must reconcile what happened and resume follow-up. This prototype explores a clearer, traceable route from intake to a CRM decision.

Designed scenario only · not an actual customer or observed operation.

  • Inconsistent validation

    Required details and routing can vary across handoffs.

  • Duplicate CRM entries

    Repeated manual entry creates a risk of duplicate records.

  • Hidden exceptions

    Integration failures need a visible, recoverable path.

Process Design & Architecture

03 / LOCAL IMPLEMENTATION

A controlled path from intake to CRM

The local prototype uses FastAPI and Pydantic for structured lead intake and applies explicit rules before any CRM write.

  1. 01

    Receive & validate

    Accept structured JSON and check required business fields.

  2. 02

    Route & qualify

    Apply deterministic sales/support rules; send invalid or ambiguous cases to human review.

  3. 03

    Write safely

    Call a separate local Mock CRM over real REST/JSON HTTP.

  4. 04

    Persist & recover

    Keep separate SQLite records, traces and audit events; failures stay recoverable.

Reliability safeguards

  • Same lead ID and same payload → idempotent replay; no second CRM write.
  • Same lead ID with a changed payload → HTTP 409; the original is not overwritten.
  • CRM outage → pending/retryable state followed by explicit manual replay.

Two Docker Compose services · only the app is bound to localhost; the Mock CRM stays on the private network.

05 / TECHNICAL CHOICE

Rules where the inputs are known

Structured fields and explicit decision criteria make deterministic validation easier to explain and test. Invalid information and ambiguous intent stay with a person instead of being guessed by a model.

“ai_candidate” is a review label only. The runtime makes no LLM calls; AI-assisted tools were used during development.
