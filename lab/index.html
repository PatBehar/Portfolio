01 · BUSINESS OPERATIONS & AUTOMATION

Process Surgeon

Download .md↓

What it does

Reviews an existing SOP or workflow, traces friction to the supplied evidence, and proposes a small, reversible improvement.

What you provide

  • Current steps, owners, handoffs and exceptions; the business goal and constraints; and any dated timing, volume or quality data.

How it works

  1. Map the current steps, owners, inputs and decisions.
  2. Trace handoffs, rework, queues and exceptions to their sources.
  3. Compare process, rules-based automation and AI-assisted options.
  4. Prioritize a small change and define a reversible pilot.

What you get

  • An evidence-linked process diagnosis, friction register, proposed future workflow, prioritized fixes and pilot checks.

Example

Example inputs

Synthetic example: a support team receives requests by email, checks details manually and re-enters them into two tools. The team wants fewer incomplete handoffs.

Expected outcome

A current-state map, the handoff gaps tied to supplied steps, a proposed owner/checkpoint, and a pilot with a baseline and stop condition. No savings are claimed without measured data.

How to use

Download the SKILL.md. Share an SOP or process description, the desired outcome and constraints. Ask for a diagnosis and a reversible pilot. Confirm owners and approvals before changing the process.

Keep in mind

Without source evidence, it can prepare discovery questions but cannot confirm root causes or estimate savings.

02 · BUSINESS OPERATIONS & AUTOMATION

Automation Architect

Download .md↓

What it does

Turns a business workflow into an automation blueprint with clear system boundaries, permissions, exception paths and human approvals.

What you provide

  • The current process and desired outcome; verified system/API documentation and permissions; data boundaries, owners and approval rules.

How it works

  1. Classify each step as manual, rules-based, workflow, model-assisted or agent-led.
  2. Map triggers, states, data and the sequence of work.
  3. Describe integration contracts, marking unverified details as proposed.
  4. Set exception, approval and security controls, then scope a small pilot.

What you get

  • A current-to-future workflow, an integration contract matrix, safeguards, pilot checks and a rollback plan.

Example

Example inputs

Synthetic example: an operations team copies approved orders into an internal system and sends status updates. System documentation is available for only one of the tools.

Expected outcome

A workflow design that separates the documented integration from the proposed one, identifies missing permissions and defines a synthetic pilot with exception handling.

How to use

Download the SKILL.md. Provide the workflow, goal and authoritative system documents. Ask for an automation blueprint. Have system owners verify access and approve any live write before implementation.

Keep in mind

Undocumented APIs, permissions or integrations remain unverified. The skill designs a plan; it does not deploy automations.

03 · AI & CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

CX Friction Finder

Download .md↓

What it does

Finds recurring friction in customer tickets or journey notes, counts it within the supplied sample and suggests practical improvements.

What you provide

  • Redacted tickets, transcripts or journey notes with stable IDs; the time period, channels and customer-experience goal.

How it works

  1. Check the sample and note missing context or bias.
  2. Group recurring friction while preserving source IDs.
  3. Separate observed patterns from possible causes.
  4. Suggest improvements and a way to test them.

What you get

  • A friction map with examples and sample counts, evidence-linked recommendations and a pilot measurement plan.

Example

Example inputs

Synthetic example: ten anonymized support messages cover a two-week period. Customers ask repeatedly where to find an order update.

Expected outcome

A recurring order-status theme with the relevant message IDs and count, a clearly labeled hypothesis about discoverability, and a small navigation test to consider.

How to use

Download the SKILL.md. Share anonymized customer feedback and explain the period, channels and goal. Ask for recurring themes and evidence-linked fixes. Review privacy and the limits of the sample.

Keep in mind

Findings describe only the material provided; a small or biased sample does not establish overall prevalence or cause.

04 · AI & CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

Agent Inspector

Download .md↓

What it does

Inspects agent instructions, available tools and supplied traces to identify observable risks and design safe, reproducible checks.

What you provide

  • The agent's prompt or instructions, expected behavior, tool descriptions and redacted traces or test results.

How it works

  1. Map allowed actions, permissions and approval boundaries.
  2. Compare expected behavior with the supplied evidence.
  3. Design safe checks for failures and prohibited actions.
  4. Classify findings and recommend focused corrections.

What you get

  • A permissions map, evidence-linked findings, a test matrix and recommended changes for human review.

Example

Example inputs

Synthetic example: an agent is expected to draft an order update but not send it. A redacted trace shows it attempted to call a send tool without approval.

Expected outcome

A high-priority finding tied to the trace, a safe reproduction check, and a recommendation to require approval before sending. No live action is taken.

How to use

Download the SKILL.md. Provide the instructions, tool permissions, expected behavior and redacted evidence. Ask for a review and safe test plan. Run proposed checks only in an approved test environment.

Keep in mind

Without traces or results, a behavior remains unverified. The skill does not change or operate a live agent.

05 · DIGITAL PRODUCTS & BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

KPI Investigator

Download .md↓

What it does

Checks KPI definitions and source data, performs reproducible calculations and distinguishes observed changes from hypotheses.

What you provide

  • A KPI definition, structured or CSV data, the reporting period, inclusion rules and source details.

How it works

  1. Check the metric definition, fields, dates and data quality.
  2. Recalculate rates and comparisons using the stated rules.
  3. Report missing or invalid values and reconcile totals.
  4. Separate observed movement from explanations that still need investigation.

What you get

  • Reconciled calculations, data-quality notes, evidence-backed findings and clearly labeled next questions.

Example

Example inputs

Synthetic example: weekly SLA results are 180 of 200 requests, then 168 of 200. No staffing, channel or demand data is supplied.

Expected outcome

The rate changes from 90% to 84%, a decrease of 6 percentage points. The change is observed; its cause remains unknown without additional evidence.

How to use

Download the SKILL.md. Provide the KPI definition and source data. Ask for a calculation and reconciliation, then review the stated rules and investigate hypotheses with additional sources.

Keep in mind

A calculation cannot repair missing or unreliable source data or prove causality. Some metrics need a clearer definition or more context.

06 · DIGITAL PRODUCTS & BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

Solution Shaper

Download .md↓

What it does

Turns a business need into a bounded MVP plan with user flows, acceptance checks and a practical build handoff.

What you provide

  • The problem, intended users, desired outcome, constraints, available tools and known risks or assumptions.

How it works

  1. Clarify the user problem and evidence behind it.
  2. Define what belongs in the MVP and what stays out.
  3. Map the main user flow and important exceptions.
  4. Write observable acceptance checks and a validation plan.

What you get

  • A focused product brief, MVP scope and exclusions, user journeys, acceptance criteria and handoff questions.

Example

Example inputs

Synthetic example: a small team wants to reduce repeated scheduling work. No user interviews, usage data or system integrations have been supplied.

Expected outcome

A limited scheduling MVP proposal, a user flow and testable checks; demand, integration needs and launch readiness remain open questions.

How to use

Download the SKILL.md. Describe the problem, users, constraints and available evidence. Ask for a first-version scope and acceptance criteria. Validate assumptions with users before committing to a build.

Keep in mind

The plan does not prove user demand, build or deploy a product, or verify unprovided costs and integrations.
