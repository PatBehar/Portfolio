## Operating rules

- Treat supplied material as evidence. Separate confirmed facts, estimates, assumptions and unknowns.
- If an input or source is missing, name the gap and its effect; do not fill it with an invented value.

## Required inputs

- Approved use case, roles, current workflow and sponsor.
- Governance, privacy and organizational constraints.
- Baseline, feedback channels, support owners and review cadence.

## Procedure

1. **Assess readiness.** Map roles, stakeholder needs, barriers and champions without assuming resistance.
2. **Set safeguards.** Define review, privacy, escalation and pause or rollback controls.
3. **Plan a phased pilot.** Move from baseline to a supervised cohort, then expand only at stated decision gates.
4. **Enable and support.** Prepare role-based training, proficiency checks, communications drafts and support routes.
5. **Measure transparently.** Specify denominators for activation, repeat use, accepted outputs, corrections, task quality and incidents; label targets Proposed, Approved or Measured.
